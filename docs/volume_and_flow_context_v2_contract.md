cohort/sector breadth, price/flow relationships and structure/participation joins.

`technical_participation_relationship` records view identity, technical version,
clean concept values/pointers and correlated-evidence keys, participation measure,
state and knowledge stage. Measure types distinguish:

| Measure | States | Reference |
|---|---|---|
| NATIVE_RELATIVE_STATE | EXPANSION / CONTRACTION / STABLE / UNDEFINED_ZERO_REFERENCE / UNKNOWN | Technical last 20 including current |
| PRIOR_20_TRAJECTORY_TREND | RISING / FALLING / FLAT / UNKNOWN | Each point relative to its prior 20 |

The trajectory uses 25/40 compatible canonical observations for five/twenty
points. It retains exact ratios, states, streaks, end-half means, acceleration
difference and own-history ECDF. Required observations and continuity are explicit.
Zeros do not manufacture a ratio. Missing dates, incompatible source/unit/basis,
CA restrictions and incomplete W/M constituents block the affected calculation.

Stale daily and older completed W/M can describe their own retained history.
Current breadth admits only current D or current/immediately previous completed
W/M with usable primitives and identical source/unit/basis semantics. W/M groups
have separate timeframe denominators. Every group and sector reports cohort,
eligible and coverage denominators, exclusions and expansion numerator. Native
volumes are not summed across unlike instruments or undocumented units.
