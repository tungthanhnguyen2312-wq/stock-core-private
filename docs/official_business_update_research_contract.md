# Official business updates — Current Research V1

Offline, explicit inputs only. No acquisition, source-route activation, published
Daily change or completed-session mutation is authorized by this capability.

## Existing owners and minimal seams

`official_source_registry.admit` owns host AND document-type admission; callers
pass its exact source/URL/type-bound verdict as `route_admission`. Missing or
mismatched verdicts fail closed. The package does not import a root registry.
HPG's host
approval does not admit operating news. `official_document_store` remains the
immutable retention owner; this library never writes to it. The existing
`official_issuer_disclosure/v1` projection supplies document metadata. Its
METADATA_ONLY qualification is preserved; registry presence proves no fact.

`stocklookup_core/evidence/business_update_observations.py` validates supplied
manifest records, UTF-8 bytes and explicit extracted observations. It reads no
runtime defaults and fetches nothing. `observe` distinguishes operating updates,
preliminary earnings, formal financial statements, management guidance and
third-party reports. Unsupported types stay UNKNOWN. A financial-statement
disclosure may return an existing-owner referral only after source/byte/time
checks; no accounting facts or valuation inputs are extracted here. Preliminary
earnings require PRELIMINARY_UNASSURED and explicit accounting scope; they never
become audited statements. Third-party reports remain discovery only.

The Corporate Intelligence axis accepts explicit observation envelopes through
`business_update_observations`. Its existing EARNINGS_BUSINESS_UPDATE category
labels this separate non-voting explanatory context. Existing event status,
materiality, catalyst counts, forward-driver qualification, freshness and posture
rules are not enlarged. No corporate-action execution dates are fabricated.

Existing forward-driver transport carries the context into Integrated Decision,
the Current Research CORPORATE dimension and evidence-axis context. The canonical
decision packet, Investment Decision Workspace and Current Research AI packet
copy it as `business_update_context`. They compute no new business facts. The
adapter is issuer-neutral; HPG identifiers appear only in research tests.

## Input and temporal contract

Manifest: ticker, issuer_name, source_id, document_id, source_url, document_type,
content_sha256, independent published_at, observed_at, known_at, assurance and
optional supersedes_document_id. Timestamps require explicit timezone offsets.
Missing, naive, later-than-cutoff or impossible knowledge time blocks research
facts. The existing decision cutoff is session 15:00 Vietnam time. Reprojection
at an earlier cutoff excludes later observations and amendments. No T0/PIT claim
is made by a current research observation.

Each observation includes native label/value/unit, deterministic decimal value,
concept, metric_kind, explicit product/accounting basis, sector applicability,
native period label and source period proof, optional proven covered-date
endpoints, value_relation (EXACT/GREATER_THAN/APPROXIMATE), exact character span
and limitations. Span bytes/text and recorded SHA must match supplied input.
Native decimal commas are handled without monetary-scale inference. Named unit
translations preserve tonne/million-tonne and currency scales. Production and
sales, percentages and absolute quantities, Q3 and cumulative nine months remain
distinct. Unproven date endpoints remain null and explicitly limited. No computed
growth comparisons are enabled; explicit YoY/QoQ comparisons retain their proof.

Content identities bind the whole envelope; tampering fails. Repeated capture of
the same document/hash/fact does not duplicate facts. Different bytes under one
document identity remain conflicted. Same metric/period/product/unit with
contradictory values is unresolved; no preferred announcement is chosen.
Supersession requires a usable later same-source/same-issuer amendment and one
unambiguous prior document. Original envelopes remain in the explanation; earlier
cutoffs cannot see corrections. These are projections, not a new persistent registry.

## Research and AI boundary

FACT preserves source-bound observations. CONTEXT preserves explicit comparison.
UNCERTAINTY carries blockers/conflicts; THESIS_IMPLICATION and COUNTER_THESIS are
research questions about demand, mix, pricing, margins and cash conversion.
DECISION_USE is NON_VOTING_RESEARCH_CONTEXT_ONLY. Sales growth never proves profit
growth, favorable facts never imply BUY, and AI supplies no numerical authority.
Posture, decision identity, valuation methods and private portfolio authority
remain unchanged. A conflicted context retains evidence for review while setting
research_usable=false.

Research fixtures use synthetic bytes and a fixture hash, coverage_kind
OFFLINE_FIXTURE, official_qualified=false and accounting_fact_authority=false.
They may demonstrate research transport despite an unapproved document type;
this explicit test/demo option never upgrades a source route. Raw missing bytes,
hash mismatch, missing identities and timing errors still block fixture facts.
Synthetic fixture bytes must never be represented as retained issuer HTML.

The October12 HPG locator was reconfirmed on October13. Q3 versus nine-month
production/sales and HRC lower bounds are represented in the offline demonstration.
Its exact original byte hash and original observation cutoff are unavailable.
One verified research locator is not a new qualified production input. The other
document classes are adversarial synthetic cases, not a Grok acquisition corpus.
Source activation and real evidence admission require separate Owner decisions.
