## Scope

context, from the same admitted exact-field stream and the same D-1/tier/entity/cutoff gates:
earnings economics (revenue, parent and total profit, explicitly sourced non-operating
components), cash conversion (operating cash flow against profit of the same document, scope,
duration and currency), earnings quality (cash/profit sign divergence, source-supported
exceptional components, missing components as UNKNOWN) and comparability (assurance, scope,
consolidated/separate, currency/unit, duration, knowledge time and source-declared
accounting-scope changes). Deterministic methods carry explicit labels. No threshold, score,
annualization, TTM, free-cash-flow, net-cash or business-quality conclusion. Financial-sector
entities never receive industrial cash-conversion interpretation.

A closed issuer gate registry records source-declared comparability cautions and exact
qualification blockers. It only refuses or explains comparisons; it never supplies an amount
to a consumer, vote, valuation or overlay. GrokBot Issue #30 values remain candidates: an
isolated offline preview may demonstrate the interface; default paths never read them.
