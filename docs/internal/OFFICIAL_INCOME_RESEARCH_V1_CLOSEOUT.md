retain their own periods, qualification, source feature tier and computed freshness;

the new stream. Matching binds issuer, metric/line/accounting definition, assurance,

same year-over-year calendar bounds. Conflicts refuse comparison. No annualization,
TTM, conversion, normalization of exceptional earnings or business-quality inference.

produce no percentage growth. Original fact/assurance/document/source-unit fields
remain available. Knowledge eligibility at the research cutoff does not establish
publication time or historical PIT. Freshness uses the existing financial-clock
policy with explicit source end dates, independently of current prices.

retained separately. UNKNOWN for the new exact-field stream never erases them.
