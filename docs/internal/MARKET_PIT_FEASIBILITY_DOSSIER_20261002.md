## Why the full historical product is deferred

The retained 269-session/248,506-pair descriptive inventory is useful research input,
but its existence does not reconstruct historical knowledge. A broad 1,683-name
Integrated Decision replay would need financial statements and revisions as actually
published/received, the denominator and valuation inputs known at each cutoff,
listing/delisting/board history, source price/volume basis, corporate terms/lifecycle
and complete factors. Period ends, folder dates, today's issuer list and later-restated
statements cannot fill those gaps. HNX/UPCoM bulk rights remain a separate cost/access
constraint. Large acquisition/parser effort would still leave unknowable historical
availability and yield misleading research comparisons.

Prospective accumulation has a clearer payoff: the existing full-product T0 journal
keeps fundamental, financial-quality, valuation, corporate, macro/sector, technical,
strategy, portfolio and AI/human context when actually available. The market foundation
adds durable low-level receipts independent of optional product outages. Costs are
bounded, lineage is auditable and future learning uses actual policy/features rather
than reconstructing them from hindsight. Genuine bounded historical market/CA research
can proceed only where its exact evidence and economic purpose justify the effort.
