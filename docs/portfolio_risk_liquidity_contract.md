# Phase 4C Risk, Liquidity and Sizing Contract

Market return risk requires a qualified adjusted-price lineage; raw/unknown basis fails closed. Liquidity average volume is independent and may be available only with qualified volume units and provenance. Beta/correlation require an aligned qualified benchmark/window and remain unavailable otherwise. Concentration and position sizing require explicit holdings, weights, cash and risk budget; none are inferred. No recommendation, sizing output, or portfolio action is emitted.
