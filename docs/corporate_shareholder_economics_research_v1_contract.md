## Economic identities

Contractual product repurchase is not an equity buyback. Its amount may be unquantified.
Booked provision and management estimate are independent; no subtraction estimates an
unbooked remainder. Profit plans remain plans. Approved proposals, registered offers and
executed issuances are separate. Execution requires its own qualified proof; a conversion can
be noncash. The generic executed-issuance reason no longer asserts fresh financing cash.

Subscription entitlements, issued, treasury, voting/outstanding, listed, tradable and EPS
weighted-average shares retain separate dates and meanings. The last requires a duration.
A bounded observation survives later continuity gaps. Existing share-proxy verdicts are
descriptive metadata, never a new current-outstanding assertion. No totals, dilution ratios,
EPS, BVPS, P/B or factors are calculated by the original V1 projection. Planned proceeds and earmarked repayments do not
become cash received or debt repaid; receipt/repayment needs distinct settlement evidence.

Warnings explain why a low P/B or technical breakout does not settle obligations, establish
attractive current equity value or repay debt. No automatic AVOID or new policy/risk vote
follows. Unknown dimensions stay explicit and local. Corporate event states and coverage,
technical outputs, posture, risk veto, investment policy, valuation eligibility, portfolio
restrictions and decision identities remain owned by their existing engines.
